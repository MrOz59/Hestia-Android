Hestia Android 0.7.0 is the **first stable release** of Hestia, the Android client for [Hermes](https://github.com/MrOz59/Hermes) built on Artemis and Moonlight. One APK runs on phones, tablets and Android TVs. It is released together with [Hermes 0.7.0](https://github.com/MrOz59/Hermes/releases/tag/v0.7.0) and went through five release candidates.

## Highlights

- **Phones, tablets and TVs in one app.** Android TV, Google TV and compatible Fire TV devices get an automatic TV layout with remote navigation, focus you can see, manual host addition and Menu/long-press OK context menus. A single universal APK covers 32-bit and 64-bit ARM and x86, and normal 2D streaming no longer needs OpenGL ES 3.0.
- **Stream info when a stream connects.** For a few seconds, an overlay says how Hermes serves the stream: what it captures, the size, rate and codec, the encoder and GPU, and the network latency. It takes no touches, so your input keeps reaching the stream. Needs Hermes 0.7.0.
- **Send a diagnostic report from the PC menu.** Long-press a paired, online Hermes PC and choose **Send diagnostic report** to save or share the host's report. Needs Hermes 0.7.0.
- **PyroWave** (experimental). Valve's low-latency wavelet codec, for 64-bit ARM devices with a compatible Vulkan GPU on a fast local network. See *Using PyroWave* below.
- **Pairing that recovers.** Overlapping attempts are prevented, incomplete sessions are cancelled after errors, and only the final confirmation is retried after a network hiccup. An abandoned PIN wait times out after three minutes. Late network-discovery callbacks on Android 14+ no longer crash pairing. Use Hermes 0.7.0 for the matching server fixes.
- **Fewer permissions.** No phone or storage permissions and no accessibility service. Keyboard shortcuts are captured natively on Android 16.1 and newer.
- **Smaller fixes.** LAN discovery is more resilient, manual IPv6 addresses are parsed correctly, and the advanced settings sections expand and collapse in place, with D-pad focus kept.

## Which APK should I install?

**If you are unsure, install `Hestia-Android-nonRoot-universal.apk`.** It works on phones, tablets and Android TVs and contains all four architectures. Install only one APK.

| Download | Choose it for |
| --- | --- |
| **`Hestia-Android-nonRoot-universal.apk`** | **Recommended.** Phones, tablets, Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV |
| `Hestia-Android-nonRoot-arm64-v8a.apk` | A smaller download for 64-bit ARM Android, used by most current phones |
| `Hestia-Android-nonRoot-armeabi-v7a.apk` | 32-bit ARM Android, common on older phones and many TVs, even some with a 64-bit processor |
| `Hestia-Android-nonRoot-x86.apk` | 32-bit x86 Android devices and emulators |
| `Hestia-Android-nonRoot-x86_64.apk` | 64-bit x86 Android devices and emulators |

"nonRoot" only means that no root is needed: these are the regular APKs. Hestia needs Android 5.0 or newer. TVs running Samsung Tizen or LG webOS cannot install APKs and need an Android device plugged in.

## Installing

1. Download the APK on the phone, tablet or TV and open it. Allow installs from that app if Android asks.
2. Open **Hestia**, add your PC, pair it and pick an app to stream.

Stable Hestia (`com.mroz59.hestia`) is a **separate app** from Hestia Nightly and the release candidates (`com.mroz59.hestia.nightly`). It does not replace them, and they do not share paired PCs or settings: pair your PC again in Hestia. You can keep both, and both install alongside Artemis and Moonlight.

Future stable releases update this one in place. To follow them in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add this repository with prereleases off.

`SIGNING_CERT_SHA256.txt`, `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` identify the signing certificate, the exact source and build, and the checksums. They are not apps to install.

## Using PyroWave

1. In Hestia: **Settings → Advanced Settings → Change codec settings → PyroWave (Experimental, SDR LAN)**.
2. Enable PyroWave in Hermes too and turn HDR off.
3. Start at **720p and 60 FPS** on a fast local network.

PyroWave needs 64-bit ARM Android and a compatible Vulkan device. It is in the universal APK and the arm64-v8a APK. The usual codecs work on every architecture and are still the ones to use for HDR. On Android, PyroWave is not zero-copy.

## Known limitations

- Building and signing checks cannot guarantee that Play Protect accepts the app (#16). If Android warns you, please report the exact message, the APK and your device.
- On Android older than 16.1, some system shortcuts stay with Android instead of reaching the PC.
- The stream info and diagnostic report need Hermes 0.7.0. With an older Hermes or another host, the overlay doesn't appear and the report option says the host gave no report.
